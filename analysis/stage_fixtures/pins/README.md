# Test files for the pin method (release 1.5)

Inputs and expected results for anything that implements section 11 of [`docs/stage-algorithm.md`](../../../docs/stage-algorithm.md): reading a place from text, the track of a recording, snapping a place to it, and the stage made from two pins.
As in the parent folder ([`../README.md`](../README.md)), **the expected results were made by the Android app's own Java code** (`android/tests/test/app/tracklog/PinFixtures.java`) from the inputs, and the Android tests regenerate them and fail if the committed files differ (`PN.FX1`).
[`../../pin_compare.py`](../../pin_compare.py) `selftest` reads the same inputs with a second implementation that was written from the method page alone and checks that it reproduces every expected result. What these files do **not** yet hold a second implementation to is in [`GAPS.md`](GAPS.md).

**Recordings** are reduced session files, as in the parent folder: the header, `L` rows and events only. A case names one by its path relative to `analysis/stage_fixtures/` (for example `road1/session_20261013_100000.csv` or `pins/outback.csv`), or `REAL` for the real Sepang recording
(`data/sepang-2026-10-13/session_20261013_095638.csv`, relative to the repository). Lines that name `REAL` are checked only when that file is available. Every recording is read as in section 1 of the method page (a recording's fixes are the `L` rows).

**Escapes.** In a text column, every UTF-16 code unit that is not printable ASCII (`0x20`-`0x7E`), and the backslash, is written `\uXXXX` (lower-case hex), so a tab or a line break inside a text never breaks a line.
**Comparing.** A number printed with a fixed number of decimals: accept your own value if it differs from the printed one by no more than half a unit of the last printed place (plus 1e-9). Text (categories, ids, file lines) must be identical. Lines starting with `#` are comments.

| File | One line is | Expected |
|---|---|---|
| `coords.tsv` | `text` TAB `result`: a text a driver might type or paste (section 11.8) | `ok LAT LON` (9 decimals) if it is read as a place, `refused` if it is not (the sentence is not part of the fixture) |
| `tracks.tsv` | `recording` TAB `vertices` TAB `length_m`: the track of a recording (11.1) | the number of vertices, and the length of the track in metres (3 decimals); `no_drive` in place of both if the recording holds no drive |
| `passes.tsv` | `recording` TAB `lat` TAB `lon` TAB `passes`: a place (7 decimals) snapped to the track of the recording (11.2) | the passes in order, `s:offset` (3 decimals each) joined by `;`, or `none` |
| `cases.tsv` | `case` TAB `recording` TAB `start` TAB `finish` TAB `result`: the two pins (11.3 to 11.6); `start` and `finish` are `pos:METRES` (a position along the track, 3 decimals) or `at:LAT,LON` (a place, 7 decimals) | `ok`, or the category of the refusal: `wrong_way`, `near_end`, `too_short`, `loop`, `hole_start`, `hole_finish`, `break`, `guard_start`, `guard_finish`, `no_pass_start`, `no_pass_finish`, `no_drive` |
| `stages/<case>.txt` | the stage file built for an `ok` case, with id `pin_<case>`, name `Pin case <case>` and `created_utc_ms 1791885600000` | the file, byte for byte up to the tolerance above (format: [`docs/stage-format.md`](../../../docs/stage-format.md)) |
| `runs/<case>.txt` | the first run found when the source recording is scored against that stage as read back from its file (section 6; run ids and the file as in the parent folder), for an `ok` case where there is one | the run file |

The recordings made for this folder (the others come from `road1/`, `road2/` and the real log): `outback.csv` (up the mountain road and back down the other lane, 14 m to the side: two passes of every place, the second one the other way round; places on the way up are 14 m from the way down, which the 10 m rule of 11.2 is about),
`parked.csv` (two minutes parked before the drive with no speed reported in the fixes, which are kept, and two minutes after it with a speed of about 0, which are left out), `badfix.csv` (every 7th fix 40 m out and reported with accuracy 45 m, every 13th with no accuracy, 40 s with no speed or course),
`short9.csv` (9.6 m of driving: a track of four vertices, but no stage), `nodrive.csv` (a minute parked with a speed of about 0 in every fix: no drive), and `stack_start_<w>.csv` and `stack_finish_<w>.csv`
(a switchback road whose legs are `w` = 12, 16, 20 and 30 m apart; the pins are in the middle of the first leg and 1.5 km on, or 1.4 km in and in the middle of the last leg: the guard of 11.6 refuses 12 and 16 and accepts 20 and 30).
`edges.csv` (twelve fixes along a line, each at an edge of the cleaning rule of 11.1: accuracy 30.0, 30.1 and none; speed 0.99, 1.0 and none; steps of 2.9, 3.1, 2.95 and 3.05 m: 8 vertices, 146.150 m), `vertices2.csv` and `vertices3.csv` (two vertices are no drive; three are a track of 30.000 m),
and `lanes.csv` (a straight road east and back west along a line 11 m to the side, noise-free: a place 0.75 m from the first lane has two passes, 0.746 m and 10.254 m away, which the 10 m rule of 11.2 keeps, because they differ by 9.5 m; a place 0.25 m from it has one, because the other is 10.5 m further).
The five `join_*.csv` are one straight road driven in two pieces in one recording, for the hole and break rules of 11.3: `join_wait300.csv` and `join_wait301.csv` have nothing for exactly 300 s and for 301 s between the pieces, and 600 m of road between them (a break needs *more* than 300 s *and* more than 100 m);
`join_jump99.csv` and `join_jump101.csv` have nothing for 400 s and a car 99.5 and 100.5 m further on; `join_still.csv` has nothing for 400 s with the car standing still (a phone that slept: a hole of any length at one place is not a break).
`road1/session_20261013_100000.csv` is the same road driven twice the same way, joined by a straight line; `road1/session_20261013_103000.csv` has a false start (forwards, back, forwards again); `road1/session_20261013_110000.csv` has a 12 s hole in the GPS; `road2/` is a hairpin road whose legs are 30 m apart and 250 m long.

Cases are named by their recording: `d_` the clean single drive `road1/session_20261013_111500.csv`, `a_` the road driven twice, `b_` the false start, `c_` the hole, `h_` the hairpin road, `o_` `outback.csv`, `p_` `parked.csv`, `x_` `badfix.csv`, `s_` `short9.csv`, `n_` `nodrive.csv`, `gf_<w>_` and `gs_<w>_` the guard at the finish and at the start, `real_` the real log, `j300_` and `j301_` the pieces with a wait of 300 s and 301 s, `jj99_` and `jj101_` the pieces with a car 99.5 and 100.5 m further on, `jstill_` the car standing across the wait.
In `c_` the 12 s hole is tried with the start pin and the finish pin before, in the first part of, in the middle of, in the last part of and after it (`hole_start` and `hole_finish` for those in it), and `c_across_300` has pins on either side of the 300 s wait between the two drives of that recording: exactly 300 s is not a break, so it is accepted.
